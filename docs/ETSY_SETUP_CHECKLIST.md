# Etsy shop setup checklist (shift-leadership-printables)

Goal: turn the existing "Baylands Farming" Etsy shop into a digital-download shop for shift and leadership printables, in under an hour.
Work through it top to bottom. Each step has a rough time.

Note: Etsy changes its screens and rules often. If a menu or number here doesn't match what you see, trust Etsy's current screen.

---

## Before you start (5 min)
- [ ] Have your ID, bank account details and phone to hand. Etsy may ask you to verify them.
- [ ] Open Canva in another tab.
- [ ] Get the first product file ready: the Shift Handover Sheet as a print-ready PDF (A4 and US Letter). If it isn't finished, you can still do steps 1 to 3 and 5 to 6, and save the listing as a draft.

## 1. Rename the shop (5 min)
Shop Manager > Settings > Info & Appearance > Shop name.
- [ ] Pick a name: 4 to 20 characters, no spaces, not already taken. Etsy limits how often you can rename, so choose once.
- [ ] Ideas to check: `ShiftLeaderStudio`, `ShiftReadyPrints`, `HandoverAndHuddle`, `FloorLeaderPrints`, `ShiftBoardStudio`.
- [ ] Update the shop title (the one-line tagline), for example: "Printable shift handover sheets, huddle boards and team leader tools".

## 2. Banner and shop icon in Canva (15 min)
- [ ] **Shop icon (logo):** 500 x 500 px. Use a simple initials mark or a clipboard icon on one solid brand colour (navy or charcoal with a yellow accent reads as "workplace").
- [ ] **Banner:** 3360 x 840 px. Show the shop name, the tagline, and 2 or 3 product mockups on the right. Keep the text in the middle, because Etsy crops the edges on mobile.
- [ ] Use the same two colours and one font on both, so the listings look like one brand.
- [ ] Upload both under Info & Appearance.
- [ ] Only use Canva elements your plan licenses for products you sell (Pro elements need Canva Pro). Canva's content licence allows selling designs that are substantially your own, not a template you haven't changed.

## 3. About section and shop announcement (5 min)
- [ ] Shop announcement: "Instant-download printables for shift leaders, supervisors and team managers. Print at home or at work."
- [ ] About: 3 to 4 sentences on who the printables are for and that they're built from real leadership training experience (Leadership by Design).

## 4. First listing: Shift Handover Sheet (15 min)
Shop Manager > Listings > Add a listing. Choose **Digital files** as the listing type.
- [ ] **Photos (5 to 10):** a mockup of the sheet on a clipboard, a close-up of a section, the "what you get" list, both page sizes, and one of it in use. Minimum 2000 px on the shortest side.
- [ ] **Title (up to 140 characters; keep the key words first):**
  `Shift Handover Sheet Printable | Shift Change Report Template | Supervisor Handover Form | A4 & Letter PDF | Instant Download`
- [ ] **Description:** start with one line on what it solves ("Stop losing information between shifts."), then what's included (file names, sizes, page count), how to use it, "digital download, nothing is shipped", and "colours may vary by printer".
- [ ] **AI disclosure:** if AI helped create the design or text, say so in the description, as Etsy's rules require.
- [ ] **Tags (13 max, 20 characters each):** shift handover, handover sheet, shift change form, supervisor tools, team leader, shift report, shift log printable, manager printable, handover template, nurse handover, warehouse shift, shift notes, printable pdf
- [ ] **Category:** Paper & Party Supplies > Paper > Calendars & Planners (or the closest "Templates" category Etsy suggests).
- [ ] **Price:** as a starting point, about USD 3 to 5 for a single sheet, and USD 9 to 15 for a bundle of 5 or more. Etsy charges in your shop currency and shows buyers theirs. Look at 3 to 5 competing listings before you save.
- [ ] **Digital files:** upload up to 5 files of 20 MB or less each (A4 PDF, Letter PDF, and optionally an editable Canva link as a PDF).
- [ ] Publish it, or save it as a draft if the file isn't final. Each listing costs USD 0.20 and renews every 4 months.

## 5. Payments and billing (10 min)
Shop Manager > Finances > Payment settings.
- [ ] Connect Etsy Payments with your bank account details. Check which options Etsy offers for South Africa on that screen.
- [ ] Add a card for Etsy fees (listing fees, a 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing fees). Etsy may charge a one-time shop setup fee in some countries.
- [ ] Complete any tax or identity verification Etsy asks for.

## 6. Shop policies (5 min)
Shop Manager > Settings > Policies.
- [ ] **Returns:** digital downloads can't be returned. Add: "If a file is faulty or you can't download it, message me and I'll fix it or refund you."
- [ ] **Processing:** instant download after payment.
- [ ] **Licence:** "For personal use or use in your own workplace. Don't resell, share or redistribute the files."
- [ ] **Privacy:** use Etsy's standard privacy policy template.
- [ ] **FAQs:** "What size is it?", "Can I edit it?", "Can I print multiple copies for my team?" (yes, within one workplace).

## 7. Final check (5 min)
- [ ] View the shop as a buyer, on your phone as well as desktop.
- [ ] Buy nothing, but open the listing and check that the photos, price and download description are right.
- [ ] Tell the Printables agent the new shop name and link. It will draft the next listings in the same format every day.
