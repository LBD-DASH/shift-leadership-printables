### 1. BUILD TODAY: Shift Handover Sheet (editable Canva template + PDF)
**For:** warehouse, manufacturing, security and retail-ops shift supervisors handing over between shifts (day/night/swing). Secondary: care-home and hospital unit leads (keep wording non-clinical, so no medical-record claims).

**Formats:** A4 (210×297 mm) and US Letter (8.5×11 in), portrait, 12 mm margins, printer-friendly (white background, one accent colour, no full-bleed fills). Fillable-by-hand line height ≥ 8 mm.

**Page 1: Shift Handover (front)**
- Header strip: `SHIFT HANDOVER` · fields: Site/Area ___ · Date ___ · Shift: ☐ Day ☐ Swing ☐ Night ☐ ______ · Outgoing lead ___ · Incoming lead ___ · Handover time ___
- Box A: **Safety first** (top, red-accent border): "Any injuries, near misses or hazards this shift?" ☐ None ☐ Yes → details + "Reported to / ref no." line. "Open safety actions the next shift must own:" 3 lines.
- Box B: **Output vs plan** table, 4 rows × columns: Line/Team | Target | Actual | Gap reason.
- Box C: **Equipment & systems** table, 5 rows × columns: Asset | Status (●OK ◐Watch ○Down) | Issue | Ticket/Who's on it | ETA.
- Box D: **People**. Staffing: Planned ___ Actual ___ · Absences/late ___ · Overtime approved ___ · "Anyone who needs a check-in (new starter, tough shift, conflict)?" 2 lines.
- Box E: **Top 3 for the next shift** (numbered, bold lines): "If you do nothing else, do these."

**Page 2: Open items & sign-off (back)**
- **Open items log** table, 8 rows: # | Item | Owner | Due | Status (Open/Handed over/Closed).
- **Customer / quality / stock notes**, 4 lines.
- **Escalations made this shift** (Who called · About what · Outcome), 3 rows.
- **2-minute verbal handover prompts** (small side panel): "What surprised you? · What's at risk? · What did you promise someone? · What would you check first?"
- **Sign-off:** Outgoing ______ Incoming ______ · ☐ Walked the floor together ☐ Read and understood.

**Page 3: How to use (buyer guide, 1 page)**: how to edit in Canva (change logo, site name, accent colour, add/remove rows), printing tips, suggested workflow (fill last 20 min of shift → 5-min face-to-face → file or photo to team chat), plus a filled-in example of page 1 (warehouse, fictional data).
