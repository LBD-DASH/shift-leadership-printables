# Printability critique — Weekly Team Check-in Sheet layout (2026-09-29)

Reviewing the Page 1 / Page 2 layout brief in `logs/2026-09-29.md` for print usability and what a real first-time team lead needs. Five specific fixes before this goes into Canva.

1. **Box B's round-robin grid has too many columns for one printed width.** Five columns (Name, Energy 1–5, Top priority this week, Blocker, Help needed from) across a portrait page with 12mm margins leaves roughly 186mm of usable width. Name needs write-in room, the Energy scale alone needs five ≥7mm circles plus gaps (about 45–50mm), and the three open-text columns (priority, blocker, help needed from) each need enough width to be legible, not just wide enough for the header word. As drafted this is tight to the point of forcing tiny columns or a page-width overflow — either cut a column (fold "Blocker" and "Help needed from" into one combined field) or move Energy to its own narrow strip and confirm actual mm widths before building in Canva.

2. **No row-height budget was written against the page.** The brief lists a header strip, Box A (3 lines), Box B (8 rows at ≥8mm plus a header row), Box C (3 lines), Box D (5 rows at ≥8mm), Box E (1 line) and a footer, but never totals that against A4's usable height (roughly 273mm inside 12mm margins). Box B alone is close to 70mm before section labels and spacing are added. Do the same arithmetic the 1:1 critique did on 2026-09-28 before export, or the layout risks coming out oversized and needing a rebuild.

3. **The Energy 1–5 scale has no anchor legend.** Unlike the Monthly Pulse on Page 2, which spells out 1 = strongly disagree and 5 = strongly agree, Box B's Energy circles give no indication of which end is good. Eight team members filling this in without a shared definition will produce numbers the facilitator can't compare or act on. Add a one-line legend under the grid header, for example "1 = running on empty, 5 = fully energised."

4. **The Actions table has no way to show whether last week's actions closed.** Box D is Owner / What / Due with no "Done" or "Carried over" column, and nothing on the sheet references the previous week's open actions. Exactly the gap flagged in the 1:1 critique: without a visible link back to last week, actions raised in one huddle can quietly die before the next one instead of being carried forward or closed off.

5. **No "next check-in" field on the sheet itself.** The footer only has Facilitator and Date. The single most common reason a recurring weekly stops happening is that the room breaks up without a locked date for the next one written down anywhere on the page. Add a "Next check-in: ___" field next to the sign-off, the same fix already applied to the 1:1 template's Day 3 brief.
