### 1. BUILD TODAY: Shift Incident / Issue Log (print-and-write PDF; Canva layout brief)

**Who it's for:** warehouse, manufacturing, security and retail-ops shift supervisors who need a one-page log when something goes wrong mid-shift (safety near miss, equipment failure, quality stop, customer complaint, staffing incident). Secondary: care-home and site leads. Keep wording non-clinical and non-HR-system claims. Pairs with the Shift Handover Sheet so open incidents carry cleanly into the next shift.

**Style match:** same as Shift Handover Sheet and 1:1. Print-and-write PDF first (no editable Canva-link claim unless Kevin confirms Pro + licence). Layout in Canva for production, then export print-ready PDFs.

**Brand:** navy #0F1F2E, teal #2A7B88, gold #C8A864, cream #F8F6F1. Headings: Playfair Display. Body: Source Sans 3. White page background, thin navy rules, teal section labels, gold accent on headers only. Printer-friendly (no full-bleed fills). Handwriting line height ≥ 8 mm. Portrait, 12 mm margins.

**Formats:** A4 (210×297 mm) and US Letter (8.5×11 in). Target files: `products/shift-incident-log/shift-incident-log-A4.pdf` and `...-Letter.pdf`.

**Page 1 - Incident / Issue Log (one incident per sheet)**

| Zone | Layout |
|---|---|
| Header strip | Navy bar with cream title `SHIFT INCIDENT / ISSUE LOG`. Gold thin underline. Fields (Source Sans 3, ≥ 8 mm lines): Site/Area ___ · Date ___ · Time found ___ · Shift: ☐ Day ☐ Swing ☐ Night ☐ ______ · Reported by ___ · Role ___ |
| Box A | **Type** (teal label). Tick boxes in one row (wrap if needed): ☐ Safety / near miss · ☐ Equipment · ☐ Quality / customer · ☐ Staffing / people · ☐ Other: _____. Prompt under: "One primary type. If it spans two, pick the one that needs action first." |
| Box B | **What happened** (teal label). Prompt: "Facts only. What did you see or hear? Who was involved (roles, not gossip)?" Four blank write-in lines (≥ 8 mm). |
| Box C | **Immediate action taken** (teal label). Three blank lines. Prompt: "What did you do right away to make it safe or stop the loss?" |
| Box D | **Impact** table, 1 row × columns: People affected \| Output / quality hit \| Equipment status (●OK ◐Watch ○Down) \| Est. downtime / delay. Row height ≥ 8 mm. |
| Box E | **Root cause (best guess now)** (teal label). Prompt: "Process · Tool · Training · Communication · Other." One line for the guess + one line "Evidence / why." |
| Box F | **Escalation** (gold-accent left rule). Fields: Escalated to ___ · How (call / radio / ticket) ___ · Time ___ · Ticket / ref no. ___. Tick: ☐ Not escalated (reason: ___). |
| Box G | **Actions to close** table, 5 rows × columns: Owner \| What \| Due \| Status (Open / Done). Row height ≥ 8 mm. |
| Footer | Sign-off: Reported by ___ · Supervisor reviewed ___ · Date ___. Thin teal note: "Carry open actions into the Shift Handover Sheet. Do not leave them only on this page." Small LBD line: Leadership by Design · leadershipbydesign.co |

**Page 2 - Open incidents tracker (bring to every handover)**

| Zone | Layout |
