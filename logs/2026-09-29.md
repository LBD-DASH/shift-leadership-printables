**Who it's for:** new team leads and retail/ops/warehouse supervisors running a 15-minute weekly huddle with a floor team (about 4-8 people). Secondary: care-home and office team leads. Keep wording non-clinical and non-HR-system claims.

**Style match:** same as Shift Handover Sheet and 1:1. Print-and-write PDF first. Layout in Canva for production, then export print-ready PDFs.

**Brand:** navy #0F1F2E, teal #2A7B88, gold #C8A864, cream #F8F6F1. Headings: Playfair Display. Body: Source Sans 3. White page background, thin navy rules, teal section labels, gold accent on headers only. Printer-friendly (no full-bleed fills). Handwriting line height ≥ 8 mm. Portrait, 12 mm margins.

**Formats:** A4 (210×297 mm) and US Letter (8.5×11 in). Target files: `products/weekly-team-check-in/weekly-team-check-in-A4.pdf` and `...-Letter.pdf`.

**Page 1 - Weekly Team Check-in (the huddle sheet)**

| Zone | Layout |
|---|---|
| Header strip | Navy bar with cream title `WEEKLY TEAM CHECK-IN`. Gold thin underline. Fields (Source Sans 3, ≥ 8 mm lines): Team ___ · Week of ___ · Facilitator ___ · Site/Area ___ |
| Box A | **Wins this week** (teal label). Prompt: "Name three things that went well." Three blank write-in lines. |
| Box B | **Round-robin grid**, 8 rows × columns: Name \| Energy 1-5 \| Top priority this week \| Blocker \| Help needed from. Energy uses five small circles (diameter ≥ 7 mm). Row height ≥ 8 mm. |
| Box C | **Decisions made** (teal label). Three blank lines. |
| Box D | **Actions table**, 5 rows × columns: Owner \| What \| Due. |
| Box E | **What we'll stop doing** (gold-accent left rule). One write-in line. Prompt: "One habit or task the team agrees to drop this week." |
| Footer | Sign-off: Facilitator ___ · Date ___. Small LBD line: Leadership by Design · leadershipbydesign.co. Teal note: "15 minutes. Stand if you can. Everyone speaks once." |

**Page 2 - Monthly pulse (optional but include in the PDF)**

| Zone | Layout |
|---|---|
| Title | `Monthly team pulse` (Playfair Display, navy). Subline: "Run once a month at the end of a check-in. Tally the scores." |
| Header | Team ___ · Month ___ · Facilitator ___ |
| Statements | Five statements, each with circles 1 2 3 4 5 (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree): (1) I know what's expected of me this week. (2) I can get help when I'm stuck. (3) We deal with problems early. (4) Wins are noticed, not only mistakes. (5) I would recommend this team as a place to work. |
| Tally strip | Average / most common score per statement (write-in). One line: "One thing we'll change next month: ___." |
| Footer | Same LBD line. Note: "This is a team conversation tool, not an employee rating form." |
