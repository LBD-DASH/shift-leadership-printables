### 1. BUILD TODAY: Feedback Conversation Log (print-and-write PDF; build-ready spec)

**Who it's for:** first-time managers, team leaders and shift supervisors (warehouse, retail, hospitality, contact centre, care and site teams) who need a short, fair written record of a praise, corrective or coaching conversation between formal 1:1s. Companion to the One-on-One Meeting Template; part of the New Manager Starter Toolkit (section 3). It is not an HR case file, disciplinary form or performance rating, and it says so on the page.

**What changed from the 2026-10-04 outline (and why):**
- Added **Before you talk** (one-point prep line + Type ticks in one box): a single-row box cannot hold a teal label plus prompt (the label-column assert fails at 9.5 mm), so Type moves into this 2-row box.
- Added **Their view** (3 lines): makes it a two-way conversation record, which is the main originality point against generic "employee feedback forms".
- Follow-up gets a **Result** tick (Better / Same / Needs another talk) and initials instead of a bare date.
- Page 2 index goes from 12 to **15 rows** (12 rows would leave 14.5 to 16 mm rows; 15 rows give 11.5 to 12.7 mm, still roomy) and the type codes are fixed: the old "P/C/C" plus "Ch" was ambiguous. Codes are now **P / C / CH**, printed in each row to circle.
- Page 2 ends with a **Month-end count** (P, C, CH tallies) and the tip "Aim for more P than C."
