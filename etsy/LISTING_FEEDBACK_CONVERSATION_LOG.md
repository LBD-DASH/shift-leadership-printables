Feedback that is fair, short and written down.

Most new managers give feedback on the fly and forget what was said. Then the next conversation starts from memory, and memory is not fair to anyone. This Feedback Conversation Log gives you one page per conversation: decide your one point before you talk, write the facts, the behaviour you saw and its impact, note how they see it, and agree small next steps with owners and dates. Page 2 is a monthly index, so you can see at a glance who you have praised, corrected or coached.

WHAT YOU GET
• 2 print-ready PDF files, 2 pages each:
  - feedback-conversation-log-A4.pdf (A4, 210 x 297 mm)
  - feedback-conversation-log-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 x 11 in)
• Page 1: one feedback conversation per sheet. Page 2: monthly index with 15 rows, a type key (P, C, CH) and a month-end count.
• Write-in lines and table rows are at least 8 mm tall, with 12 mm margins and no full-page colour fills, so it prints well on a home or office printer.
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

WHAT'S ON THE SHEET
• Header: team member, role, date, manager, team or shift, time
• Before you talk: the one thing you want them to keep doing or change, and the type (praise, corrective or coaching)
• Situation: when and where, facts only
• Behaviour: what you saw or heard yourself
• Impact: on the team, the customer, the work or safety
• Their view: how they see it, in their words
• What we agreed: 4 rows for action, who, by when and done
• Follow-up: follow-up date, result (better, same, needs another talk) and initials
• Page 2: manager, team or shift and month, then a monthly index (date, team member, type, topic, follow-up date, done) and a month-end count of praise, corrective and coaching conversations

HOW TO USE IT
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print at home or at work, in colour or black and white. Print at "actual size" / 100%.
3. Before the conversation, fill in the one thing and tick the type.
4. During or straight after, write the facts, their view and what you agreed.
5. Add one line to Page 2, and check back on the follow-up date.

LICENCE
• For personal use, or for use within one workplace (a single site or business). Print as many copies as that workplace needs.
• Please don't resell, share, upload or redistribute the files, or use them in products for sale.

RETURNS AND FAULTY FILES
Because this is a digital download, returns and exchanges aren't accepted. If a file is faulty or won't download, send me a message and I'll fix it or refund you.

PLEASE NOTE
• This is a print-and-write PDF, not an editable template. There is no Canva, Word or Google Docs version.
• It is a conversation record for good day-to-day management. It is not an HR, disciplinary, legal or performance rating form, and it does not replace your employer's policies.
