### Description

Stop losing information between shifts.

Most handover problems aren't people problems. They're routine problems. When there is no set way to hand over, the important things — the near miss, the machine that's acting up, the task that's half done — live in someone's head and leave with them at the end of the shift.

This one-page Shift Handover Sheet gives outgoing and incoming leaders the same structure every time. Fill it in, walk through it together, sign it off. Five minutes. Nothing missed.

**WHAT YOU GET**
• 2 print-ready PDF files, 1 page each:
  – shift-handover-sheet-A4.pdf (A4, 210 × 297 mm)
  – shift-handover-sheet-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in)
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

**WHAT'S ON THE SHEET**
• Date, shift (day / night / other), outgoing leader, incoming leader, area / line
• Staffing: planned vs actual headcount, absences, overtime and cover
• Safety & incidents: incidents and near misses, open safety actions and hazards to watch
• Production / output vs target: measure, target, actual, variance, reason for variance
• Equipment & maintenance: asset, issue, job number / status
• Open tasks carried over: task, owner, due date, status
• Quality or customer issues
• Key messages for the next shift (numbered lines: 5 on A4, 4 on US Letter)
• Handover sign-off: outgoing and incoming leader signatures and times

**HOW TO USE IT**
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print it at home or at work, in colour or black and white. Print "actual size" / 100% for best results.
3. The outgoing leader fills it in during the last part of the shift.
4. Walk through it face to face with the incoming leader, then both sign it off.
5. File it, or pin it to the board for the next shift. Print a fresh sheet every shift.
