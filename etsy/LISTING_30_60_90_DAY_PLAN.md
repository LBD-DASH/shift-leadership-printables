## Proposed page plan (for the PDF build)
- **Page 1, Plan on a page:** name, new role, team or shift, start date, manager. "By day 90 I want...": three outcomes. People I must meet: 8-row list (name, role, date met). How I'll know it's working: three signs.
- **Page 2, The three phases:** three columns. Days 1-30 "Listen and learn": meet every team member, learn the shift routines, handover, safety rules and numbers, no big changes yet. Days 31-60 "Fix the basics": start regular 1:1s, set one team routine, sort two quick wins. Days 61-90 "Lead and improve": agree team goals, deal with one hard issue, plan the next quarter. Each column: 5 tick-box actions, 2 write-in lines for "what I learned", and a phase check-in date with my manager.
- **Page 3, 13-week check-in:** one row per week: what I did, what I learned, what's next, energy 1 to 5.
- Portrait, print-and-write, same palette and fonts as the other products, 8 mm or more line height, 12 mm margins.

Your first 90 days as a manager, on three pages.

Most new team leaders were promoted because they were good at the job. Then day one arrives and nobody tells them what to do first. This New Manager 30-60-90 Day Plan gives you a simple order: listen and learn for 30 days, fix the basics by day 60, and start leading and improving by day 90. Write it down, check it every week, and walk into your 90-day review with proof of what you did.

WHAT YOU GET
• 2 print-ready PDF files, 3 pages each:
  - new-manager-30-60-90-day-plan-A4.pdf (A4, 210 x 297 mm)
  - new-manager-30-60-90-day-plan-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 x 11 in)
• Page 1: your plan on a page. Page 2: the three phases. Page 3: a 13-week check-in.
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

WHAT'S ON THE PAGES
• Plan on a page: your role, team or shift, start date, three outcomes for day 90, the people you need to meet, and three signs it's working
• Days 1 to 30, Listen and learn: meet every team member, learn the routines, the handover, the safety rules and the numbers
• Days 31 to 60, Fix the basics: start regular 1:1s, set one team routine, get two quick wins
• Days 61 to 90, Lead and improve: agree team goals, deal with one hard issue, plan the next quarter
• Each phase: tick-box actions, space for what you learned, and a check-in date with your own manager
• 13-week check-in: what I did, what I learned, what's next, energy 1 to 5

HOW TO USE IT
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print at home or at work, in colour or black and white. Print at "actual size" / 100%.
3. In your first week, fill in Page 1 and share it with your manager.
4. Work through Page 2 one phase at a time. Tick actions as you finish them.
5. Spend five minutes on Page 3 every Friday. Take all three pages to your 30, 60 and 90-day conversations.
