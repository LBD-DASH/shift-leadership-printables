Stop losing information between shifts.

Most handover problems aren't people problems. They're routine problems. When there is no set way to hand over, the near miss, the machine that's acting up and the task that's half done live in someone's head and leave with them at the end of the shift. This one-page Shift Handover Sheet gives the outgoing and incoming leader the same structure every time. Fill it in, walk through it together, both sign. Five minutes. Nothing missed.

WHAT YOU GET
• 2 print-ready PDF files, 1 page each:
  - shift-handover-sheet-A4.pdf (A4, 210 x 297 mm)
  - shift-handover-sheet-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 x 11 in)
• Write-in lines and table rows are at least 8 mm tall, with 12 mm margins and no full-page colour fills, so it prints well on a home or office printer.
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

WHAT'S ON THE SHEET
• Header: date, shift (day, night or other), handover time, outgoing leader, incoming leader, area or line
• Staffing: planned and actual headcount, absent or late, overtime or cover arranged
• Safety: incidents and near misses, hazards to watch, open safety actions
• Output vs target: 3 rows for measure, target, actual, variance and reason
• Equipment: 3 rows for equipment or asset, issue, job number and status
• Open tasks: 4 rows for task, owner, due and status
• Quality and customers: complaints, defects, holds
• Key messages: the top three things the next shift must know
• Sign-off: outgoing and incoming leader, with times

HOW TO USE IT
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print at home or at work, in colour or black and white. Print at "actual size" / 100%.
3. The outgoing leader fills it in during the last part of the shift.
4. Walk through it face to face with the incoming leader, then both sign.
5. File it, or pin it to the board for the next shift. Print a fresh sheet every shift.

LICENCE
• For personal use, or for use within one workplace (a single site or business). Print as many copies as that workplace needs.
• Please don't resell, share, upload or redistribute the files, or use them in products for sale.

RETURNS AND FAULTY FILES
Digital downloads can't be returned for a change of mind. If anything is wrong with your PDF (it won't download or open, the page size is wrong, a page is missing or text is unreadable), message me with your order number and I'll refund you in full. You can keep the file.

PLEASE NOTE
• This is a print-and-write PDF, not an editable template. There is no Canva, Word or Google Docs version.
• Pair it with the Shift Incident Log for full details of anything that goes wrong mid-shift.
• Colours may look slightly different depending on your screen and printer.
