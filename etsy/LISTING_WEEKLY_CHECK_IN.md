```
A 15-minute team check-in that ends with owners and dates.

Most weekly team meetings drift. The loudest people talk, actions get lost, and nobody remembers what was agreed last week. This Weekly Team Check-in gives new team leaders the same 15-minute routine every week: check last week's actions, name three wins, go round the team once (energy, top priority, blocker), write down decisions and actions with owners, agree one thing to stop doing, and set the next check-in before anyone leaves. Page 2 is a monthly team pulse: five short statements the team scores together, so you notice drift before it becomes a problem.

WHAT YOU GET
• 2 print-ready PDF files, 2 pages each:
  - weekly-team-check-in-A4.pdf (A4, 210 x 297 mm)
  - weekly-team-check-in-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 x 11 in)
• Page 1: the weekly huddle sheet, with a round-robin grid for up to 8 people. Page 2: the monthly team pulse.
• Write-in lines and table rows are at least 8 mm tall, with 12 mm margins and no full-page colour fills, so it prints well on a home or office printer.
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

WHAT'S ON THE SHEET
• Header: team, site or area, facilitator, week of
• Last week: actions closed, carried over and needing escalation
• Wins this week: three numbered lines
• Round-robin: 8 rows for name, energy (circle 1 to 5, with a legend), top priority this week, and blocker and help needed from
• Decisions made: two lines
• Actions: 5 rows for owner, action, due date and a done tick box
• Stop doing: one habit or task the team drops this week
• Next check-in: date, time and where
• Suggested timings for each part, adding up to 15 minutes
• Page 2: team, month and facilitator; five team statements scored 1 to 5 with tally columns, this month's average and last month's average; what we heard (no names); a check-ins this month table (week of, present, closed, carried, what we stopped doing); and one change for next month with an owner and a check date

HOW TO USE IT
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print at home or at work, in colour or black and white. Print at "actual size" / 100%.
3. Before the check-in, fill in the header and last week's actions.
4. In the room, follow the sheet from top to bottom. Everyone speaks once in the round-robin.
5. At the last check-in of the month, run Page 2: one tally mark per person for each statement, then pick the lowest line and agree one change.

LICENCE
• For personal use, or for use within one workplace (a single site or business). Print as many copies as that workplace needs.
• Please don't resell, share, upload or redistribute the files, or use them in products for sale.

RETURNS AND FAULTY FILES
Because this is a digital download, returns and exchanges aren't accepted. If a file is faulty or won't download, send me a message and I'll fix it or refund you.

PLEASE NOTE
• This is a print-and-write PDF, not an editable template. There is no Canva, Word or Google Docs version.
• The monthly pulse is a team conversation tool. It is not an employee rating, engagement survey or HR form, and it does not replace your employer's policies.
• Colours may look slightly different depending on your screen and printer.
```
