### Description

Stop running weeklies that go nowhere.

A good team check-in is short, everyone speaks once, and it ends with owners and dates. This Weekly Team Check-in Sheet gives new leads the same 15-minute routine every week: wins first, a round-robin on energy and blockers, decisions, actions, and one thing the team will stop doing. Page 2 is a monthly pulse so you notice drift before it becomes a crisis.

**WHAT YOU GET**
• 2 print-ready PDF files (huddle sheet + monthly pulse in each size):
  – weekly-team-check-in-A4.pdf (A4, 210 × 297 mm)
  – weekly-team-check-in-Letter.pdf (US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in)
• Instant download after purchase. Nothing is shipped.

**WHAT'S ON THE SHEET**
• Header: team, week of, facilitator, site/area
• Wins this week: three write-in lines
• Round-robin grid: name, energy (1–5), top priority this week, blocker, help needed from
• Decisions made: three write-in lines
• Actions table: who, what, by when
• What we'll stop doing: one write-in line
• Sign-off: facilitator and date
• Page 2: monthly pulse — five team-health statements rated 1–5, a tally strip, and one change to make next month

**HOW TO USE IT**
1. Download the PDF in the size you need (A4 or US Letter).
2. Print at home or at work, colour or black and white. Print "actual size" / 100%.
3. Fill Team and Week of beforehand. Leave Wins and the grid for the room.
4. Go round the grid once. Capture decisions and actions. Agree one thing to stop.
5. Once a month, run Page 2 and tally the scores together.
